Hi, I'm MaxJubayerYT

Also known as MaxDev. I'm a YouTube content creator, Minecraft modder and Android developer from Bangladesh.

I lead CopperLauncher, a Minecraft: Java Edition launcher for Android forked from MojoLauncher. It's the thing that lets you play Minecraft Java on a phone, and most of my time goes into keeping it from breaking.

Copper-Android CopperLauncher site

What I'm up to

Contributing to Copper-Android and PojavLauncher-Reborn, and making the landscape UI better in Copper-Android. Outside the launcher I publish modpacks and mods on Modrinth.
